Built-state claims below are based on the Terraform and exported-state excerpts available in the engagement notes. Where the excerpt or executed evidence is insufficient, the conclusion is marked accordingly rather than inferred as fact.

## Harmless or limited differences

The architecture describes aggregate reporting dashboards in analytics; the supplied evidence does not establish that the name or workload inventory changed. No severity is assigned for naming or age alone. The app ingress security groups in `sg-app-00.tf` through `sg-app-05.tf` allow TCP/443 only from `10.0.0.0/8`, not from the public internet. This is broader than a workload-specific source range, but the supplied evidence does not show a direct route from that rule to the telemetry data; treat it as a bounded hardening item, not the headline.

## Overall conclusion

The most consequential disagreement is the combined trust and data path: the build identity can enter the production deployment role; the role has deployment capability; and the associated node-reader identity can read operational-restricted telemetry. The current credential-to-role-to-reader-to-object path was executed against the account API, and the activity record independently contains successful cross-account assume-role, deployment update, and object-read events on 2026-10-19. The repository credential commit is dated 2026-10-28 and its live caller ARN is a user ARN while the export names a role ARN, so it is not attributed to the earlier session and the identity discrepancy remains for the client to reconcile.
