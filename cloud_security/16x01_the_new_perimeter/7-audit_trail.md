## Detection point

The highest-value alert is row 4, the successful `AssumeRole` into `ashford-crossacct-reader`: it is when the original principal becomes the role whose policy was modified. Role assumptions are routine, so alerting on that role name alone would be noisy. Correlate the source ARN `user/reporting-analyst`, target role ARN, role session name and STS-issued access-key identity with a preceding `CreatePolicyVersion`/`SetDefaultPolicyVersion` against a policy attached to that role. Raise priority when the new default expands to wildcard actions or resources and the role assumption follows within seconds. The normal path is an assume-role without a just-modified attached policy; the unusual sequence is policy mutation immediately followed by assumption and privileged IAM use.
