# 9. Review: The Refusal

The most revealing refusal was `aws iam create-access-key --user-name reporting-analyst`, which returned `AccessDenied` even though `ReportingAnalystAccess` explicitly allows `iam:CreateAccessKey` in `SelfKeyManagement`. The governing layer is the organization SCP `AshfordGuardrails`, statement `NoLongLivedKeys`, which explicitly denies `iam:CreateAccessKey` and `iam:CreateLoginProfile` for every resource. CloudTrail records the refused `CreateAccessKey` at `2026-11-19T03:27:32Z` and `03:44:49Z`; the call response names `reporting-analyst`. An SCP caps permissions for principals in member accounts, so the identity-policy allow cannot override the SCP's explicit deny.

The handover says the control was added after the 2024 review because Ashford intended to move to federation and prevent identities from minting long-lived keys. The organization was worried about persistent credentials surviving their intended task or escaping normal federation controls. That concern is justified: the estate still has legacy service users with active keys, while a no-key governance statement did not stop either of the two tested privilege compositions. The policy-version chain escalated to administrator without creating a key, and the service-role chain delegated an administrator role without one. The control addresses credential persistence but not privilege reachability. The broad pipeline trust also weakens the intended assurance of federation because its audience condition does not constrain the subject to Ashford's own deployment workflow.
