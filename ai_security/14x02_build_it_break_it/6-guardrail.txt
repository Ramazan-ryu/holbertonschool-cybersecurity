# Task 6 — Deploy a Guardrail and Measure It

**Guard and class:** Input filter on the `message` channel, aimed at prompt injection and instruction extraction. Normalization and decode scanning were enabled; three deny patterns were configured; the model-based guard was disabled.

**Measurement:** `guardrail_harness.py --seed 42` tested all 60 mutated prompt-injection variants. 13 were blocked and 47 bypassed: **47/60 = 78.3% bypasses**. The supplied benign set had **0/30** blocked requests.

**Bypasses:** Every tested fake-delimiter (5/5), multilingual (6/6), semantic-paraphrase (8/8), indirection (6/6), and benign-lookalike (5/5) variant bypassed. Direct override had 4/6 bypasses, roleplay 4/5, encoding 3/7, obfuscation 2/6, payload splitting 2/3, and fake transcript 2/3. The filter caught some literal or decodable phrases, but generally missed semantic rewrites, indirect requests, and inputs without the configured vocabulary.

**Operational meaning:** On this supplied and mutated set, most attempts reached the agent. This filter is a screening layer, not an authorization boundary. The 0/30 benign result describes only the provided benign corpus and does not establish a general false-positive rate.
