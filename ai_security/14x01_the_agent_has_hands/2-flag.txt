## Root Cause Mechanism
Vector similarity search ranks chunks strictly by embedding proximity without evaluating content age, author authority, or factual accuracy. The vector store was not "tricked"; it simply returned the closest mathematical matches in its index. However, both persistence across sessions and downstream influence on an unrelated query remain unproven in this run.
