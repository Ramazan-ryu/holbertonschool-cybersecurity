**Board Pitch – MedDefense Security Investment**

**Current State**
After four weeks of assessment, MedDefense’s security posture is high-risk and exposed to preventable attacks. Critical gaps in access control, monitoring, and employee awareness leave sensitive patient data vulnerable. In simple terms: we are one incident away from a major breach.

**The Risk**
If we do nothing, a ransomware attack is the most likely scenario. An attacker could encrypt patient records and halt operations for days. Based on our Annual Loss Expectancy (ALE), a single incident could cost $350,000 in downtime, recovery, legal penalties, and reputational damage.

**The Plan**
We recommend a focused investment of $120,000 in three priority controls. First, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) to prevent unauthorized access, even if passwords are stolen. Second, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR), a system that continuously monitors devices and stops attacks in real time. Third, Security Awareness Training to reduce human error, the cause of most breaches. Together, these controls reduce overall risk exposure by approximately 70%.

**The Return**
This investment reduces expected losses from $350,000 to roughly $105,000. For every $1 invested, MedDefense avoids about $2 in potential losses while also protecting operations, patient trust, and regulatory compliance.
