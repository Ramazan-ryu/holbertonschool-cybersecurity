Risk Acceptance Memo — R7 (DDoS on public marketing website)

1. Risk: Averline accepts the residual risk of DDoS-driven downtime on the public, marketing-only website, which processes no transactions and holds no customer data.
2. Economic rationale: Annualized exposure (ALE) is 8,000 euros/year (AV 8,000 x EF 100% x ARO 1); the proposed premium anti-DDoS service costs 24,000 euros/year, a net loss of 16,000 euros/year if purchased, so the control is declined.
3. Risk appetite: An 8,000-euro annual exposure sits below the board's stated tolerance of 25,000 euros of uninsured exposure per risk, so acceptance is within appetite.
4. Owner: Head of IT Infrastructure. Review date: 21 August 2027.
5. Trigger for early review: reopen this decision immediately if the site adds transactional or customer-data functionality, hosts a scheduled product launch, or experiences an outage exceeding 4 hours.
