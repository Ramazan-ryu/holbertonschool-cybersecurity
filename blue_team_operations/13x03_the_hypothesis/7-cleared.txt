QUERIES

Share access (Security 5140, 5145), destination-side:
data.win.system.eventID:(5140 OR 5145) AND data.win.eventdata.shareName:(*ADMIN$* OR *C$* OR *IPC$*) AND (data.win.eventdata.subjectUserName:(eli.cole OR avery.eaves OR jude.pike) OR data.win.eventdata.ipAddress:(10.0.10.31 OR 10.0.10.57 OR 10.0.10.66) OR data.win.agent.name:(NIM-ENG-031 OR NIM-ENG-057 OR NIM-ENG-066))

The agent.name clause returns share events recorded on the source hosts themselves. These are not lateral movement and are reviewed separately. The identity and IP clauses form the lateral-movement filter.

Outbound SMB connections (Sysmon Event ID 3), source-side:
data.win.system.eventID:3 AND data.win.eventdata.destinationPort:(445 OR 139) AND (data.win.agent.name:(NIM-ENG-031 OR NIM-ENG-057 OR NIM-ENG-066) OR data.win.eventdata.sourceIp:(10.0.10.31 OR 10.0.10.57 OR 10.0.10.66) OR data.win.eventdata.user:(*eli.cole* OR *avery.eaves* OR *jude.pike*))
Correlated logons, explicit credentials, and CIFS tickets (4624, 4648, 4769):
(data.win.system.eventID:4624 AND data.win.eventdata.logonType:3 AND (data.win.eventdata.targetUserName:(eli.cole OR avery.eaves OR jude.pike) OR data.win.eventdata.ipAddress:(10.0.10.31 OR 10.0.10.57 OR 10.0.10.66))) OR (data.win.system.eventID:4648 AND data.win.eventdata.targetServerName:* AND (data.win.eventdata.targetUserName:(eli.cole OR avery.eaves OR jude.pike) OR data.win.eventdata.ipAddress:(10.0.10.31 OR 10.0.10.57 OR 10.0.10.66))) OR (data.win.system.eventID:4769 AND data.win.eventdata.serviceName:*cifs* AND (data.win.eventdata.targetUserName:(eli.cole OR avery.eaves OR jude.pike) OR data.win.eventdata.ipAddress:(10.0.10.31 OR 10.0.10.57 OR 10.0.10.66)))
