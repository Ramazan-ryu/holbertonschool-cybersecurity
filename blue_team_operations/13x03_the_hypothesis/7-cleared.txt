# 7-cleared.txt

**Technique:** T1021.002 Remote Services (SMB, RDP, and WinRM-style lateral movement)

**Data sources:** Windows Security Event IDs 4624, 4648, and 4769, and Sysmon Event ID 3 (network connection). Coverage was checked across the full Nimbus index before the hunt: each of these event types returned ordinary events from other Nimbus endpoints, which confirms the channels are ingested fleet-wide.

**Scope of the search:**
- Hosts: NIM-ENG-031, NIM-ENG-057, and NIM-ENG-066 (the confirmed foothold hosts)
- Identities: NIMBUS\eli.cole, NIMBUS\avery.eaves, and NIMBUS\jude.pike
- Period: 2026-09-01T00:22:54.128Z through 2026-10-13T04:56:17.155Z
- Destination ports checked for Sysmon 3: 445 (SMB), 3389 (RDP), 5985 and 5986 (WinRM)

**Queries run:**

    data.win.agent.name:(NIM-ENG-031 OR NIM-ENG-057 OR NIM-ENG-066) AND data.win.system.eventID:(4624 OR 4648 OR 4769 OR 3)

    data.win.user:(eli.cole OR avery.eaves OR jude.pike) AND data.win.system.eventID:(4624 OR 4648 OR 4769)

    data.win.system.eventID:3 AND data.win.eventdata.destinationPort:(445 OR 3389 OR 5985 OR 5986)

**Result:** The queries returned zero matching SMB, RDP, or WinRM-style lateral-movement events from the listed foothold hosts or identities in the stated period.

**Conclusion:** Within the listed hosts, identities, period, ports, and event types, no lateral movement by these remote-service methods is observed. This is a bounded absence, not a clearance of the fleet.

**Limits of this conclusion:**
- It does not show that the three foothold hosts themselves were collecting these event types for the full period. The fleet-wide ingestion check does not establish host-level continuity, and per-host ingestion or agent-health records for the window were not reviewed.
- It does not cover movement outside the window, from other identities or hosts, or over ports and services not in the query.
- It does not rule out the technique through an unsearched method (for example, DCOM, scheduled tasks on remote hosts, or service creation over other channels).

**Confidence:** Moderate. The queries and their fields are reproducible and the event types are ingested fleet-wide. Confidence in the absence on the three foothold hosts specifically stays moderate until their collection continuity over the window is confirmed from ingestion or agent-health records.

**To raise confidence to high:** pull per-host event counts for NIM-ENG-031, NIM-ENG-057, and NIM-ENG-066 for each day in the window, for Event IDs 4624, 4648, 4769, and Sysmon 3. Any day with zero events on a host that should be active is a gap, and the absence must be re-scoped to exclude it.
