**Technique:** T1021.002 Remote Services (SMB, RDP, and WinRM-style lateral movement)

**Status:** Not cleared. The absence below cannot be attributed to the foothold hosts until their telemetry coverage for the window is verified.

**Data sources:** Windows Security Event IDs 4624, 4648, and 4769, and Sysmon Event ID 3 (network connection). Fleet-wide, each of these event types returned ordinary events from other Nimbus endpoints, so the channels are ingested somewhere in the estate. This does not show that they were ingested from the target hosts.

**Scope of the search:**
- Hosts: NIM-ENG-031, NIM-ENG-057, and NIM-ENG-066 (the confirmed foothold hosts)
- Identities: NIMBUS\eli.cole, NIMBUS\avery.eaves, and NIMBUS\jude.pike
- Period: 2026-09-01T00:22:54.128Z through 2026-10-13T04:56:17.155Z
- Destination ports checked for Sysmon 3: 445 (SMB), 3389 (RDP), 5985 and 5986 (WinRM)

**Queries run:**

    data.win.agent.name:(NIM-ENG-031 OR NIM-ENG-057 OR NIM-ENG-066) AND data.win.system.eventID:(4624 OR 4648 OR 4769 OR 3)

    data.win.user:(eli.cole OR avery.eaves OR jude.pike) AND data.win.system.eventID:(4624 OR 4648 OR 4769)

    data.win.system.eventID:3 AND data.win.eventdata.destinationPort:(445 OR 3389 OR 5985 OR 5986)

**Result:** The queries returned zero matching SMB, RDP, or WinRM-style lateral-movement events from the listed hosts or identities in the stated period.

**Conclusion:** No lateral movement by these remote-service methods was observed in the searched data. This result is not a clearance of the foothold hosts. A zero result from a host that was not logging these event types is indistinguishable from a true absence, and the coverage for the three hosts has not been checked.

**Limits:**
- Per-host ingestion over the window has not been verified. A gap on any host would make its zero result meaningless.
- Movement outside the window, from other identities or hosts, or over ports and services not in the queries is not covered.
- Methods outside these event types (for example, DCOM, remote scheduled tasks, or remote service creation over other channels) are not covered.

**Confidence:** Low for the foothold hosts. Moderate for the fleet-wide statement that these event types are ingested somewhere in Nimbus.

**Next step to clear the absence:** Count events per host, per day, for Event IDs 4624, 4648, 4769, and Sysmon 3, for NIM-ENG-031, NIM-ENG-057, and NIM-ENG-066 across 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-13. For each host:
- If every day has events, the zero result can be restated as a bounded absence for that host.
- If any day has none, that day is a coverage gap. Re-scope the absence to exclude it and state the gap in the conclusion.
