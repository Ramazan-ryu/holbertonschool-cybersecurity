Corvan’s gateway correctly evaluated PZ-0001 and allowed it: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC all passed for `harrowfield-metalworks.example` because the mail genuinely came from that supplier’s authorised relay and was signed with its valid key. This was correct behaviour for authentication controls, which establish domain authorisation and alignment rather than sender-account integrity or intent.
